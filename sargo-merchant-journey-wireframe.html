Total: 2 taps on the first batch, 1 tap thereafter. A batch of 1,000 payments is the identical journey — the product contract forbids per-order selection, confirmation, or wallet signatures after the single authorization.

3 · Banking part — the trust boundary in detail

ElementDesignWhy (safety)
Verified hostname barTrusted native chrome, outside bank content, always visiblePhishing anchor — merchant can always confirm they're on real Equity
Password + OTP entryOnly on the bank's page; Sargo renders no bank-password fields everCredentials never transit Sargo code; no autofill; no MFA automation
Navigation allowlistOnly reviewed Equity domains + approved MFA redirects openNo arbitrary URL/SSRF/redirect lanes
Session lifetimeBank session valid only inside this batch; dies at completionNo always-on bank access, no lingering cookies
Statement permissionsBank's own grant page approves read accessThe bank — not Sargo — gates every read
Post-batchSession cleanup, no persisted credentials, encrypted evidence onlyNothing reusable is left on the device
Anti-abuse visible to the merchant: ambiguous matches → human review, never auto-release; coarse-date rows → honest refusal, never an invented timestamp; every release carries a re-derivable audit receipt.
